According to Meta’s fiscal 2025 Form 10-K:

- **Fine:** €200 million for noncompliance of its “subscription for no ads” model with DMA Article 5(2), imposed in April 2025.
- **Appeal date:** July 4, 2025. [Form 10-K, pp. 54–55](<url:filing-source>)
- **2025 Europe revenue:** **US$46,569 million**, based on customer addresses.
- **Non-EU countries explicitly included in Europe:** **Russia and Turkey**. [Form 10-K, Note 2, p. 102](<url:filing-source>)
