Costco reported for fiscal 2025 (52 weeks ended August 31, 2025):

- **Membership fee revenue:** **US$5,323 million**.
- **Year-end member renewal rates:** **92.3%** in the **U.S. and Canada** and **89.8% worldwide**.

Source: [Costco fiscal 2025 Form 10-K](<url:filing-source>), “Membership Fees” (p. 26) and Consolidated Statements of Income (p. 37).
