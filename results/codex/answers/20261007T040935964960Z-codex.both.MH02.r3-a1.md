LMT’s fiscal 2025 Form 10-K reports:

- **F-35 sales share:** **27%** of total consolidated sales and **67%** of Aeronautics sales.
- **Aeronautics results:** sales of **$30,257 million** and operating profit of **$2,086 million**. [Source: 2025 Form 10-K, Items 1 and 7](<url:filing-source>)

Implied F-35 sales: **67% × $30,257 million = approximately $20,272 million ($20.3 billion)**. As a cross-check, 27% of consolidated sales of $75,048 million yields $20,263 million; the slight difference reflects rounded percentages. [Source: Form 10-K, Note 4](<url:filing-source>)
