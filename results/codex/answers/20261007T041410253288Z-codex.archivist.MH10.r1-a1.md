Costco’s fiscal 2025 Form 10-K, filed October 8, 2025, reported:

- **Membership fee revenue:** **US$5,323 million**. [Item 7](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)
- **Year-end total paid members:** **81,000 thousand**, including **38,700 thousand Executive members**. [Item 1, membership table and footnote](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)

The implied membership fee revenue per year-end paid member is **US$65.72**:
US$5,323 million ÷ 81 million members. This uses the year-end member count, not an annual average.
