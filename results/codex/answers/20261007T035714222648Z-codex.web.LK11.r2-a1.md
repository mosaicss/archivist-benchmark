Barrick’s 2025 Annual Information Form, p. 48 (“Employees and Labor Relations”), reports approximately:

- **27,000 employees worldwide**, excluding contractors.
- **32,300 contractors**.
- **13,000 employees** represented by a labor union or covered by collective bargaining agreements.

These are reported as at **December 31, 2025**, although the included Mali workforce figures are current as of June 30, 2025. [Source: Barrick’s 2025 AIF, filed with the SEC](<url:filing-source>).
