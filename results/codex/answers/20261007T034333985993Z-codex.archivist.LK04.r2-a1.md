UNP’s July 29, 2025 Form 8-K, Item 1.01, states:

- **Cash consideration:** $88.82 per Norfolk Southern share, without interest, plus one Union Pacific share. [Merger Consideration](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)
- **Regulatory termination fee:** Union Pacific would pay Norfolk Southern **$2.5 billion in cash** under specified circumstances involving failure to obtain regulatory approvals.
- **End Date before automatic extensions:** **January 28, 2028**. [Termination and Fees](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)
