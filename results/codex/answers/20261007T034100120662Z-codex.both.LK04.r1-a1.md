According to [Item 1.01 of Union Pacific’s July 29, 2025 Form 8-K](<url:filing-source>):

- **Cash consideration:** $88.82 per Norfolk Southern share, without interest, plus one Union Pacific share.
- **Regulatory termination fee:** $2.5 billion in cash payable by Union Pacific to Norfolk Southern under specified circumstances involving failure to obtain regulatory approvals.
- **End Date before automatic extensions:** January 28, 2028.
