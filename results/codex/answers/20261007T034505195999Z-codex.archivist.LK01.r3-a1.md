For fiscal 2025 (52 weeks ended August 31, 2025), Costco reported:

- **Membership fee revenue:** **US$5,323 million**.
- **Year-end member renewal rates:** **92.3%** in the **U.S. and Canada** and **89.8% worldwide**.

Source: [Costco’s Form 10-K, filed October 8, 2025, Item 7—Membership Fees](<url:mosaic-finance.com>).
