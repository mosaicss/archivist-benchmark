For 2025, the F-35 generated **27% of total consolidated sales** and **67% of Aeronautics sales**. Aeronautics reported **US$30,257 million in sales** and **US$2,086 million in operating profit**. ([2025 Form 10-K, pp. 3 and 38](<url:filing-source>))

Implied F-35 sales: **67% × $30,257 million = approximately $20,272 million ($20.3 billion)**. This is an estimate because the disclosed percentage is rounded.
