LMT’s fiscal 2025 Form 10-K reports that the F-35 generated **27% of consolidated sales** and **67% of Aeronautics sales**. Aeronautics’ 2025 sales were **US$30,257 million**, and operating profit was **US$2,086 million**. [Source: 2025 Form 10-K](<url:filing-source>)

Implied F-35 sales were **approximately US$20.3 billion**: 67% × US$30,257 million = US$20,272 million. Cross-checking against consolidated sales gives 27% × US$75,048 million = US$20,263 million; the small difference reflects rounded percentages. [Source for consolidated sales](<url:filing-source>)
