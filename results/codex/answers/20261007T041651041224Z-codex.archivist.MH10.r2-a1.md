Costco’s fiscal 2025 Form 10-K, filed October 8, 2025, reports:

- **Membership fee revenue:** **US$5,323 million**. [Item 7, Membership Fees](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)
- **Year-end total paid members:** **81,000 thousand**, including **38,700 thousand Executive members**. [Item 1, Membership](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)
- **Implied revenue per year-end paid member:** $5,323 million ÷ 81 million = **US$65.72** (calculated using year-end membership, rather than average membership).
