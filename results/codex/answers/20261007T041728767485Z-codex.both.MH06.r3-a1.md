**Intermodal** was CN’s largest commodity group, accounting for **22% of 2025 total revenues**. ([2025 annual MD&A, p. 21](<url:www.cn.ca>))

Its 2025 results were:

- **Revenues:** C$3,892 million
- **Revenue ton miles:** 52,249 million
- **International market:** 63% of intermodal revenues

([2025 annual MD&A, p. 25](<url:www.cn.ca>))
