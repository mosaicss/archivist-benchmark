Costco’s fiscal 2025 Form 10-K reports:

- **Membership fee revenue:** **US$5,323 million**.
- **Year-end total paid members:** **81,000 thousand**.
- **Year-end Executive members:** **38,700 thousand**, included in total paid members.

Source: [Costco FY2025 Form 10-K, membership table (p. 5) and income statement (p. 37)](<url:filing-source>).

Implied membership fee revenue per year-end paid member: **$5,323 million ÷ 81 million = US$65.72**. This uses the year-end member count, rather than an annual average.
