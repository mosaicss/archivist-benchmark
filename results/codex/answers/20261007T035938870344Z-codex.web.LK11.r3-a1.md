Barrick’s 2025 AIF, under “Employees and Labor Relations” (p. 48), reports approximately:

- **27,000 employees worldwide**, excluding contractors.
- **32,300 contractors**.
- **13,000 employees** represented by a labor union or covered by collective bargaining agreements.

These are the figures reported as at December 31, 2025; the included Mali workforce figures are current as of June 30, 2025. [Source: Barrick’s 2025 AIF, p. 48](<url:s25.q4cdn.com>).
