For fiscal 2025 (52 weeks ended August 31, 2025), Costco reported:

- **Membership fee revenue:** **US$5,323 million**.
- **Member renewal rates at year-end:** **92.3%** in the **U.S. and Canada**, and **89.8% worldwide**.

Source: [Costco fiscal 2025 Form 10-K, “Membership Fees,” p. 26](<url:filing-source>).
