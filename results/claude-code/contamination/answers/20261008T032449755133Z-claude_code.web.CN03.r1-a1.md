Kraken Robotics' revenue was C$16.1 million in Q1 2025 and C$21.7 million in Q1 2026. The amounts are in Canadian dollars, which is how the company reports.

- **Q1 2025: C$16.1M.** This was down 23% from C$20.9M in Q1 2024. Product revenue fell 42% to C$9.2M, and service revenue rose 38% to C$7.0M. ([Kraken Q1 2025 release](<url:www.krakenrobotics.com>))
- **Q1 2026: C$21.7M**, reported May 28, 2026. This was up 35% from Q1 2025. The company said the growth came mainly from demand for its SeaPower subsea batteries and synthetic aperture sonar, plus growth in its subsea services business. Adjusted EBITDA was C$3.0M. ([Kraken Q1 2026 release](<url:www.krakenrobotics.com>))
