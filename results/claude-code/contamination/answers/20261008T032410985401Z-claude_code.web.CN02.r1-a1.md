At December 31, 2023, Tesla held **$16.40 billion ($16,398 million) in cash and cash equivalents**, according to its FY2023 Form 10-K.

It also held $12,696 million in short-term investments. That brings cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments to **$29,094 million (about $29.1 billion)**.

This comes from a search-result summary of the 10-K, not a direct read of the filing's balance sheet. The figures match Tesla's reported FY2023 results.
