As of July 31, 2025, the end of RBC's third quarter of fiscal 2025, RBC reported:

- **CET1 ratio: 13.2%.** This is the same as at April 30, 2025 and October 31, 2024. Earnings retained after dividends (net internal capital generation) added to the ratio, but risk-weighted asset growth, share buybacks, a U.S. rating downgrade and risk parameter changes offset that gain. ([Q3 2025 MD&A](<url:mosaic-finance.com>); [Q3 2025 news release](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
- **Tier 1 capital ratio: 14.8%.** That is up 10 bps from the prior quarter, mainly because of net issuance of Additional Tier 1 instruments. ([Q3 2025 MD&A](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))

Behind those ratios, CET1 capital was C$95,654M, Tier 1 capital was C$107,155M and total risk-weighted assets were C$723,155M. The ratios are calculated under OSFI's Basel III Capital Adequacy Requirements guideline. ([Q3 2025 interim report, Regulatory capital table](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
