Kraken Robotics reported these consolidated revenues (C$, for the quarters ended March 31):

- **Q1 2026: $21.7 million ($21,705 thousand).** That's up 35% from Q1 2025. Product revenue rose 50% to $13.8M and service revenue rose 14% to $7.9M. ([Q1 2026 MD&A](<url:mosaic-finance.com>); [Q1 2026 news release](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
- **Q1 2025: $16.1 million ($16,128 thousand).** Product revenue was $9.2M and service revenue was $7.0M. The FY2025 MD&A's quarterly summary gives the same $16,128 figure. ([FY2025 MD&A, Summary of Quarterly Information](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))

The Q1 2026 increase was mainly due to SeaPower subsea battery and synthetic aperture sonar (SAS) product sales, plus 3D at Depth, which Kraken acquired in April 2025.
