As of August 30, 2025, the end of fiscal 2025, AutoZone had:

- **U.S.: 6,627 stores**
- **Mexico: 883 stores**
- **Brazil: 147 stores**
- **Total: 7,657 stores** (the three counts add up to this total)

In the fourth quarter alone, AutoZone opened 91 U.S. stores and closed one. It also opened 45 in Mexico and 6 in Brazil, for 141 net new stores.

These numbers come from AutoZone's Q4 fiscal 2025 earnings release, as quoted in the search results. I didn't open the SEC filing page itself.
