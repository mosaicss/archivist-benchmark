Tesla held **$16,398 million (about $16.4 billion)** in cash and cash equivalents at December 31, 2023, up from $16,253 million a year earlier. This comes from the consolidated balance sheet in its FY2023 10-K, filed 2024-01-29 ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).

What makes up that $16,398 million: $15,903 million in cash, $109 million in money market funds, $277 million in U.S. government securities and $109 million in commercial paper ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).

Tesla also held $12,696 million in short-term investments. Adding those gives $29,094 million in total cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments (same source).
