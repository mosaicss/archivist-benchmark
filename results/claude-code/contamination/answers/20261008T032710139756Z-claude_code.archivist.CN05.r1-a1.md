Home Depot's proxy statements list total compensation for Edward P. "Ted" Decker (Chair, President and CEO) in the Summary Compensation Table as follows:

| Fiscal year | Total | Salary | Stock awards | Option awards | Non-equity incentive | All other |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2025** | **$16,191,127** | $1,400,000 | $9,612,251 | $2,369,917 | $2,657,631 | $151,328 |
| **2024** | **$15,574,678** | $1,426,923 | $9,043,035 | $2,199,952 | $2,743,532 | $161,237 |

Sources:
- **Fiscal 2025:** the 2026 proxy (DEF 14A, filed 2026-04-07), [Summary Compensation Table](<url:mosaic-finance.com>).
- **Fiscal 2024:** the 2025 proxy (DEF 14A, filed 2025-04-07), [Summary Compensation Table](<url:mosaic-finance.com>). The 2026 proxy shows the same $15,574,678 for 2024.

Things to keep in mind when comparing the two years:
- **Equity values:** The stock and option amounts are the grant-date fair value of the awards, not cash he was paid.
- **Week count:** Fiscal 2024 had 53 weeks, so its salary includes one extra week of pay.
