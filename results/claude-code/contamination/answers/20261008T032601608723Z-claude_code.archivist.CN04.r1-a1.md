For the second quarter ended June 30, 2022, CN reported **diluted EPS of C$1.92, up 32% year over year**.

- **Press release (July 26, 2022):** "the Company reported diluted EPS up 32% to C$1.92" ([News release](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **Q2 2022 MD&A:** net income was $1,325 million, up 28%, and "diluted earnings per share increased by 32% to $1.92" ([MD&A](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **Prior-year figure:** Q2 2021 diluted EPS was $1.46 ([quarterly data table](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)). By my calculation, that is an increase of $0.46 per share. CN restated 2021 figures for a pension accounting change it made in Q1 2022, but Q2 2021 diluted EPS was $1.46 both before and after the restatement.

The adjusted figure is different: adjusted diluted EPS was a record C$1.93, up 30%.
