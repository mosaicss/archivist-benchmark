As of December 31, 2025, Chipotle owned **3,938 restaurants in the United States** and **104 restaurants internationally**, for **4,042 company-owned restaurants** in total.

- Chipotle's FY2025 10-K, filed February 4, 2026, gives these figures in Item 1, Business ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)). The MD&A overview in Item 7 repeats them ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- The 4,042 total matches the year-end count in the restaurant activity table ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)) and the figure in Item 2, Properties ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- Chipotle also had 14 international partner-operated restaurants in the Middle East, run with the Alshaya Group. These are not company-owned, so they are not in the counts above ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
