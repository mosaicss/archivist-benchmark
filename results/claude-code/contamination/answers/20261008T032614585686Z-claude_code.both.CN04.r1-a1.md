For the second quarter of 2022 (three months ended June 30, 2022), CN reported diluted EPS of **C$1.92**. That was **up 32%** from C$1.46 in Q2 2021.

- **Press release (July 26, 2022):** "the Company reported diluted EPS up 32% to C$1.92" ([News release](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **Q2 2022 MD&A:** net income was $1,325M, up 28%, and diluted EPS was $1.92, up 32% ([MD&A](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **Note 8 to the financial statements:** diluted EPS was $1.92 for Q2 2022 and $1.46 for Q2 2021 ([6-K, Note 8](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).

The 2021 comparative figure was restated because of a change in how CN accounts for pension assets in Q1 2022, but Q2 2021 diluted EPS stayed at $1.46 under both the old and new method ([quarterly data table](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)). Adjusted diluted EPS, which is a different measure from the one you asked about, was C$1.93, up 30%.
