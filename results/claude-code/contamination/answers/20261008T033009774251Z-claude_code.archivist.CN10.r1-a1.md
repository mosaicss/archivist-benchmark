AutoZone's fiscal 2025 Form 10-K (filed 2025-10-27) gives these store counts as of August 30, 2025:

- **U.S.:** 6,627 stores
- **Mexico:** 883 stores
- **Brazil:** 147 stores

The same numbers appear in [Item 1, Business](<url:mosaic-finance.com>), in [Item 7, MD&A](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) and in [Note A to the financial statements](<url:mosaic-finance.com>).

They add up to 7,657 stores, which matches the total in [Item 2, Properties](<url:mosaic-finance.com>): 4,307 leased and 3,350 owned.
