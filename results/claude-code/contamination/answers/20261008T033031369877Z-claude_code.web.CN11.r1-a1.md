As of December 31, 2025, Chipotle owned **3,938 restaurants in the U.S.** and **104 restaurants internationally**, for 4,042 company-owned in total. There were also 14 international restaurants run by partners, which are not counted in the company-owned numbers.

The FY2025 Form 10-K says: *"As of December 31, 2025, we owned 3,938 Chipotle restaurants throughout the United States ("U.S.") and 104 international Chipotle restaurants. Additionally, we had 14 international partner-operated restaurants."*
