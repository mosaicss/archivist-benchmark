At July 31, 2025, the end of fiscal Q3 2025, RBC reported:

- **CET1 ratio: 13.2%**, unchanged from April 30, 2025
- **Tier 1 capital ratio: 14.8%**, up from 14.7% at April 30, 2025

RBC's Q3 report to shareholders, filed with the SEC, also gives a total capital ratio of 16.6% and a leverage ratio of 4.5%. These are calculated under OSFI's Basel III Capital Adequacy Requirements guideline.

The earnings release states the CET1 figure. The Tier 1 figure comes only from the Q3 report filed with the SEC. I couldn't read RBC's own PDF of that report to cross-check it.
