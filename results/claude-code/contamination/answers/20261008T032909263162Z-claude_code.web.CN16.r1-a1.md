Nike's total demand creation expense **rose about 1%** in fiscal 2026 (year ended May 31, 2026) compared with fiscal 2025.

- **FY2026:** $4,754 million
- **FY2025:** $4,689 million
- **Change:** up $65 million. Nike reports this as +1%; the exact figure is about +1.4%.

Nike says the increase came from higher sports marketing spending and unfavorable currency exchange rates. Lower brand marketing spending partly offset it, because fiscal 2025 had extra spending on major sports events. A search summary of the 10-K says currency changes added about 2 percentage points, which would mean spending fell slightly when currency effects are removed. I couldn't confirm that figure directly because the 10-K pages wouldn't load in a readable form. The dollar amounts and the +1% come from Nike's Q4 FY2026 earnings release.
