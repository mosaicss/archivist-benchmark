NIKE's total demand creation expense **rose about 1.4%** in fiscal 2026 compared with fiscal 2025. The 10-K rounds this to 1%.

- **Dollar amounts:** It was $4,754 million in fiscal 2026 (year ended May 31, 2026) and $4,689 million in fiscal 2025, an increase of $65 million. That works out to 1.39% ([NIKE FY2026 10-K, Note 1](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **NIKE's explanation:** The management discussion says the expense "increased 1% due to higher sports marketing expense and unfavorable changes in foreign currency exchange rates, partially offset by lower brand marketing expense." Currency changes alone added about 2 percentage points, so the expense would have fallen slightly without them ([NIKE FY2026 10-K, Item 7](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).

Source: NIKE, Inc. Form 10-K filed July 15, 2026 (accession 0000320187-26-000088).
