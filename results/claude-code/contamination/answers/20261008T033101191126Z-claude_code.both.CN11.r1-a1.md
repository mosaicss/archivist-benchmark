Chipotle's 10-K for fiscal 2025, filed February 4, 2026, gives these counts as of December 31, 2025:

- **United States:** 3,938 company-owned restaurants
- **International:** 104 company-owned restaurants, in Canada and Europe

That makes 4,042 company-owned restaurants in total. The filing also lists 14 international restaurants run by a partner (the Alshaya Group in the Middle East), which aren't counted as company-owned.
