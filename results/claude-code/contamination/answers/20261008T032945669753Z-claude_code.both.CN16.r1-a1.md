NIKE's total demand creation expense **rose about 1%** in fiscal 2026 compared with fiscal 2025.

- **Amounts:** $4,754 million for the year ended May 31, 2026, up from $4,689 million in fiscal 2025. That's an increase of $65 million, or about 1.4% by my calculation ([10-K filed 2026-07-15, Item 8](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **NIKE's figure:** The MD&A rounds this to "increased 1%." It says the rise came from higher sports marketing expense and unfavorable foreign currency exchange rates, partly offset by lower brand marketing spending, since the prior year had heavier spending on key sports events. Currency changes alone added about 2 percentage points, so the expense fell slightly when currency effects are excluded ([10-K, Item 7](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
