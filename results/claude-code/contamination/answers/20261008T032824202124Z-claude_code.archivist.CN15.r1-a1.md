At July 31, 2025 (end of Q3 fiscal 2025), Royal Bank of Canada's ratios were:

- **CET1 ratio: 13.2%**, the same as the prior quarter. Net capital generated internally was offset by risk-weighted asset (RWA) growth, share buybacks, a U.S. rating downgrade and changes to risk parameters. ([Q3 2025 MD&A](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
- **Tier 1 capital ratio: 14.8%**, up 10 bps from the prior quarter, mainly because of net issuance of Additional Tier 1 instruments. ([Q3 2025 MD&A](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))

The Q3 2025 interim report's capital table shows the amounts behind these ratios ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)):

- CET1 capital: C$95,654M
- Tier 1 capital: C$107,155M
- Total RWA: C$723,155M

Both ratios are calculated under OSFI's Basel III Capital Adequacy Requirements (CAR) guideline.
