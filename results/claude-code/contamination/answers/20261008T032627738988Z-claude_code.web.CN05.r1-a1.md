Home Depot reported these totals for CEO Ted Decker:

- **Fiscal 2024: $15,574,678 (about $15.6 million).** This comes from the proxy statement filed in April 2025, which also gives a CEO-to-median-worker pay ratio of 443 to 1. ([MarketScreener](<url:www.marketscreener.com>); [FY2025 DEF 14A](<url:filing-source>))
- **Fiscal 2025: almost $16.2 million, up about 4% from 2024.** That breaks down as a $1.4M salary, about $9.6M in stock awards, nearly $2.7M in non-equity incentives and about $2.5M in options and other pay. ([AJC](<url:www.ajc.com>); [FY2026 DEF 14A](<url:filing-source>))

The fiscal 2025 number is rounded and comes from the AJC article. I couldn't get the exact dollar amount from either SEC filing because the fetch tool didn't return their Summary Compensation Tables. One search summary also attached the fiscal 2025 breakdown to fiscal 2024 by mistake, so I left out a 2024 breakdown.
