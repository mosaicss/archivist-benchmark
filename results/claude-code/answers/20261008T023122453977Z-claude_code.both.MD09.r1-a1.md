**(a) Layoff size:** The 8-K said the layoff would affect about **11,000 employees** across the Family of Apps and Reality Labs segments. The attached Zuckerberg memo describes it as "more than 11,000," or about 13% of staff. ([8-K Item 7.01](<url:mosaic-finance.com>); [Ex. 99.1](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))

**(b) Reaffirmed 2022 outlook:** Total expenses of **$85–87 billion**, which the 8-K said was "unchanged." That range included estimated Q4 severance costs and about $900 million in office-consolidation charges. ([8-K Item 7.01](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))

**(c) 2022 total costs and expenses:** **$87,665 million** for the full year ended December 31, 2022. ([10-K income statement](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))

**(d) Headcount at December 31, 2022:** **86,482**, up 20% year over year. The 10-K says this figure still includes most of the roughly 11,000 laid-off employees, who would drop out of headcount by the end of Q1 2023. ([10-K MD&A](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))

**(e) Amount above the top of the range:** **$665 million** ($87,665M − $87,000M). This is my own calculation.

The 10-K shows some restructuring charges that the 8-K outlook didn't specifically name, such as $1.34 billion in data center asset abandonment charges. These may help explain the overrun, but the filings don't directly tie them to it. ([10-K MD&A](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
