Here are the answers. I couldn't read the original KAP PDF (the text wouldn't extract), so they come from news reports that quote the disclosures.

**(a)** Koç Holding owns **about 75%** of Tek-Art. The July 2025 coverage says "yaklaşık yüzde 75 oranında pay sahibi olduğu bağlı ortaklığı Tek-Art" ("its subsidiary Tek-Art, in which it holds about a 75% stake"). A separate CNBC-e headline says Koç's stake in Tek-Art Marina reached 83.76%, but that refers to a different date, not the July 29 disclosure.

**(b)** The agreed total price was **US$160 million** before closing adjustments. It covered 100% of Beta Marina, bought from Yıldız Holding, and 50% each of MCI and RAM, bought from Sağlam İnşaat.

**(c)** The deal closed on **2 December 2025**, the same day as the KAP update. Forbes Türkiye gives 3 December, which looks like the date of its own article.

**(d)** The amount paid was **US$157,730,193** (about $157.73 million), subject to closing adjustment.

**(e)** That is **US$2,269,807** (about $2.27 million) less than the agreed price.
