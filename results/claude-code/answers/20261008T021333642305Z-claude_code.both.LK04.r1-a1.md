These figures come from Union Pacific's Form 8-K filed 2025-07-29, Item 1.01 (accession 0001193125-25-168150):

- **Cash per Norfolk Southern share:** $88.82 in cash, without interest, plus one share of Union Pacific common stock ([Item 1.01, Merger Consideration](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **Termination fee for failing to get regulatory approvals:** Union Pacific would pay Norfolk Southern $2.5 billion in cash if the agreement is terminated in certain circumstances tied to that failure ([Item 1.01, Termination and Fees](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)). Separately, either company would owe the other $2.5 billion in cases involving a change of board recommendation or a competing deal proposal.
- **End Date:** January 28, 2028. It extends automatically in certain circumstances ([same passage](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
