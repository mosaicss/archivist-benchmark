Here is comparable store sales growth in Canada for each quarter, taken from the English MD&A for that quarter:

| Quarter | Comp. store sales growth | Breakdown (transactions / avg. transaction size) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 FY2026 (13 wks to May 4, 2025) | **4.9%** | +3.7% / +1.2% | [MD&A filed 2025-06-11, Sales](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) |
| Q2 FY2026 (to Aug 3, 2025) | **4.9%** | +3.9% / +0.9% | [MD&A filed 2025-08-27, Sales](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) |
| Q3 FY2026 (to Nov 2, 2025) | **6.0%** | +4.1% / +1.9% | [MD&A filed 2025-12-11, Sales](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) |
| Q4 FY2026 (13 wks to Feb 1, 2026) | **1.5%** (3.5% without the calendar shift) | not given for the quarter | [Annual MD&A filed 2026-03-24, Key Items Q4](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) |
| Q1 FY2027 (to May 3, 2026) | **5.6%** | +3.5% / +2.0% | [MD&A filed 2026-06-11, Sales](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) |
| Q2 FY2027 (to Aug 2, 2026) | **5.4%** | +3.7% / +1.7% | [MD&A filed 2026-09-16, Results of Operations](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) |

- **Q1 FY2026:** That MD&A says "comparable store sales" without "in Canada". It came before the Australian (TRS) acquisition on July 21, 2025, so the figure covers Canada only. Starting in Q2 FY2026, the MD&As say "in Canada" and give no comparable store sales figure for Australia.
- **Q4 FY2026:** The 1.5% compares 13 weeks with 13 weeks. The 3.5% figure removes the calendar shift caused by Fiscal 2025 having 53 weeks. The full Fiscal 2026 figure was [4.2%](<url:mosaic-finance.com>). The [segment table](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) also shows 1.5%.
- **Q2 FY2027:** The Canadian [segment table](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) also shows 5.4%. The figure for the first six months of FY2027 was 5.5%.
