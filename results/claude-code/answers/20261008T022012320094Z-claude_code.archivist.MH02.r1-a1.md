In 2025 the F-35 made up **27% of Lockheed Martin's total consolidated sales** and **67% of Aeronautics' sales**. Aeronautics had **$30,257 million in sales** and **$2,086 million in operating profit**, which implies **about $20.3 billion in F-35 sales**.

**Reported in the 10-K (filed 2026-01-29):**
- **F-35 shares:** The F-35 is the company's largest program and generated 27% of total consolidated sales and 67% of Aeronautics' sales in 2025 ([Item 1](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)). Note 3 calls the 27% "approximately," and gives 26% for both 2024 and 2023 ([Note 3](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **Aeronautics, full year 2025:** sales of $30,257M (2024: $28,618M) and operating profit of $2,086M (2024: $2,523M), for a 6.9% margin ([MD&A, Aeronautics](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **Total consolidated sales:** $75,048M ([segment table](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).

**My calculation (not stated in the filing):**
- 67% × $30,257M ≈ **$20.3 billion** ($20,272M)
- As a check, 27% × $75,048M ≈ $20,263M, which matches.

Because the percentages are rounded, treat about $20.3 billion as an estimate. The 10-K doesn't give an exact F-35 sales figure, and it doesn't report F-35 operating profit at all.
