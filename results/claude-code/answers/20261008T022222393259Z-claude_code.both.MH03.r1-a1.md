Here are the answers from Pfizer's FY2025 Form 10-K, filed 2026-02-26:

- **Eliquis worldwide revenues in 2025:** **$7,961 million**, up from $7,366 million in 2024. That is $5,148M in the U.S. and $2,813M internationally ([MD&A product discussion](<url:mosaic-finance.com>); [Note 17C](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **Share of total revenues:** **13%** of Pfizer's 2025 total revenues ([Item 1A](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)). My own check: $7,961M ÷ $62,579M total revenues is about 12.7%, which matches the rounded 13%.
- **Settled generics' U.S. launch date:** **April 1, 2028** ([Item 1, patents footnote 5](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **Formulation patent expiry:** **2031** (same footnote).

The same footnote adds some context:
- The composition of matter patent expires in November 2026.
- Pfizer won its case against three other generic companies, so they cannot launch until the formulation patent expires in 2031.
- A launch before April 1, 2028 could still happen. If the formulation patent is found invalid or not infringed in future litigation, generics could launch on November 21, 2026. If both patents are found invalid or not infringed, they could launch immediately.
