All eight FY2025 10-Ks (filed in February 2026) name these auditors. My tools couldn't load the full audit-report sections of the SEC filings. Most of them came back cut short as XBRL data, and the JPMorgan PDF wouldn't parse. So I found the "auditor since" years through search results, and how directly each one is confirmed varies (see the last column).

| Company | Auditor | Auditor since | How it was confirmed |
|---|---|---|---|
| JPMorgan Chase (JPM) | PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP | 1965 | PwC is named in the FY2025 10-K. "We have served as the Firm's auditor since 1965" comes from earlier JPM 10-Ks, as reported by the search results. |
| Bank of America (BAC) | PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP | 1958 | PwC is the long-standing auditor. 1958 comes from secondary sources (CalPERS). I couldn't see the sentence in the FY2025 10-K. |
| Citigroup (C) | KPMG LLP | 1969 | The same sentence appears in Citi's 10-Ks and proxy statements year after year. |
| Goldman Sachs (GS) | PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP | 1922 | PwC audited the FY2025 statements. 1922 is long-standing disclosure that the search confirmed, but I didn't see the FY2025 text itself. |
| Morgan Stanley (MS) | Deloitte & Touche LLP | 1997 | Report dated Feb 19, 2026. Deloitte was chosen at the 1997 merger and has served since then. |
| PNC (PNC) | PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP | 2007 | Report dated Feb 20, 2026. PwC replaced Deloitte starting with the 2007 audit. |
| U.S. Bancorp (USB) | Ernst & Young LLP | 2003 | Report dated Feb 23, 2026: "We have served as the Company's auditor since 2003." EY replaced PwC in February 2003. |
| Charles Schwab (SCHW) | Deloitte & Touche LLP | 1976 | Deloitte is confirmed for FY2025. 1976 comes from the audit report for Schwab's broker subsidiary, Charles Schwab & Co., and I couldn't see it in the parent company's 10-K text. |

If you need the exact wording, check Item 8 of each linked 10-K, especially for BAC, GS, JPM and SCHW.
