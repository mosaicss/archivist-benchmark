- **Ownership of Cobre Panamá: 90% effective interest.** In November 2017 the company raised its effective ownership of MPSA, which holds Cobre Panamá, to 90% by buying LS-Nikko's 50% holding of KPMC. - **Concentrate that funds the program: 121,000 dry metric tonnes.** The program is paid for by exporting the copper concentrate stored at the site. ([2025](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)) The AIF also says carrying out the program is still waiting on approval from the Panamanian authorities. ([Risk factor, pt. 3/3](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
