**Costco (COST): answers**

**(a)** The fiscal 2024 Form 10-K (filed 2024-10-09) says Costco had **333,000 employees worldwide** at the end of 2024. That breaks down as 219,000 in the U.S., 53,000 in Canada and 61,000 in Other International. ([10-K, Item 1 – Human Capital](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))

All of (b) to (e) come from the CEO Pay Ratio section of the DEF 14A filed 2024-12-11. ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))

**(b)** At June 30, 2024 there were about **328,000 employees worldwide**, of whom about 216,800 were in the U.S. and its territories. Some countries were excluded as de minimis, which left a "considered population" of about 316,000.

**(c)** Ron Vachris's total compensation for the calculation was **$12,354,831**. Vachris became CEO on January 1, 2024, so his salary and non-equity incentive pay were annualized. That's why this figure is higher than his Summary Compensation Table total of $12,229,741. ([SCT](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))

**(d)** The median employee's total compensation was **$47,092**.

**(e)** The reported pay ratio is **262:1**. The proxy also gives a supplemental ratio that counts only full-time workers: 192:1, with a median of $64,318.
