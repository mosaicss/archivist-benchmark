All six figures come from Dollarama's English MD&As. Each is for that quarter alone (not year to date) and covers Canadian stores only. Dollarama does not report comparable store sales for its Australian segment, which it acquired in July 2025.

| Quarter | Period end | Comparable store sales growth | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 Fiscal 2026 | May 4, 2025 | **4.9%** | [MD&A filed 2025-06-11, Sales](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) |
| Q2 Fiscal 2026 | Aug 3, 2025 | **4.9%** | [MD&A filed 2025-08-27, Sales](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) |
| Q3 Fiscal 2026 | Nov 2, 2025 | **6.0%** | [MD&A filed 2025-12-11, Sales](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) |
| Q4 Fiscal 2026 | Feb 1, 2026 | **1.5%** | [Annual MD&A filed 2026-03-24, Sales](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) |
| Q1 Fiscal 2027 | May 3, 2026 | **5.6%** | [MD&A filed 2026-06-11, Sales](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) |
| Q2 Fiscal 2027 | Aug 2, 2026 | **5.4%** | [MD&A filed 2026-09-16, Sales](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) |

- **Q1 Fiscal 2026:** This MD&A came out before the Australian acquisition, so it says "comparable store sales" without "in Canada." At that point all stores were Canadian.
- **Q4 Fiscal 2026:** The prior-year quarter had 14 weeks, so the comparison was made on a 13-week basis. The MD&A says that without the calendar shift, growth would have been 3.5%.
- **Q1 and Q2 Fiscal 2027:** Each of these MD&As also restates the prior-year quarter as 4.9%, which matches the original Q1 and Q2 Fiscal 2026 figures.
