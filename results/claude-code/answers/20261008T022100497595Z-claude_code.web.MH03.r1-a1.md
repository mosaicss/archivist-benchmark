Answers from Pfizer's FY2025 Form 10-K:

1. **Eliquis worldwide revenues in 2025:** $7,961 million. That's $5,148 million in the U.S. and $2,813 million internationally.
2. **Share of Pfizer's total 2025 revenues:** about 13%. This checks out against total 2025 revenues of about $62.6 billion (7,961 / 62,600 ≈ 12.7%).
3. **U.S. generic launch date for the settled companies:** April 1, 2028. Pfizer and BMS kept litigating against three other generic companies and won, so those three can't launch until the formulation patent expires.
4. **Formulation patent expiry:** 2031. The composition-of-matter patent expires in November 2026, and the filing notes that both patents could face future challenges.
