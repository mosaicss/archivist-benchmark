In 2025, THY's Technic segment earned an operating profit of TL 13,928m, about **15.5%** of consolidated operating profit of TL 90,129m. All figures are from the Finansal Rapor filed 2026-03-04, in TL millions, for the year ended 31 December 2025.

| Item | 2025 |
|---|---|
| Consolidated revenue (Hasılat) | **955,472** |
| Consolidated operating profit (Esas Faaliyet Karı) | **90,129** |
| Technic segment total revenue | **107,220** (26,857 from outside the group + 80,363 from other segments) |
| Technic segment operating profit | **13,928** |
| Technic's share of consolidated operating profit | 13,928 / 90,129 ≈ **15.5%** (my calculation) |

- **Source of the consolidated figures:** the income statement ([passage](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **Source of the Technic figures:** Note 4.2, segment results ([passage](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)). In that note the Aviation (76,201) and Technic (13,928) operating profits add up to 90,129. The elimination column is zero on that line, so the 15.5% share needs no adjustment.
- **Revenue caveat:** about 75% of Technic's 107,220 revenue is sales to other group segments, which are removed on consolidation. So comparing Technic's total revenue with consolidated revenue would overstate its weight. Its revenue from outside the group is 26,857, or about 2.8% of consolidated revenue.
