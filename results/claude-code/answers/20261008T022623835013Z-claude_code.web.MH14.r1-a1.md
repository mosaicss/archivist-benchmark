I couldn't extract the AIF's text directly: both copies came back as unreadable PDF data. Three of the four answers are well corroborated, but the ownership figure is not confirmed.

1. **Ownership interest in Cobre Panamá: likely 90%, not confirmed.** First Quantum has held 90% of Minera Panamá S.A. (MPSA) since it bought LS-Nikko's 10% in 2017. Korea's KOMIR holds the remaining 10%. One search summary of the 2025 AIF reported **91.02%** for MPSA, apparently from the subsidiaries table. Another summary of the same AIF gave a conflicting breakdown, and I couldn't check either against the text. Look at the subsidiaries section of the AIF to confirm.
2. **P&SM approval announcement date: May 30, 2025.** The government of Panama approved MPSA's preservation and safe management (P&SM) program for the mine and instructed it to proceed.
3. **Stored concentrate: 121,000 dry metric tonnes.** The program is funded by exporting this copper concentrate, which was stored at the site.
4. **2025 copper production: about 396,000 tonnes.** This is within the revised guidance of 390,000–410,000 tonnes. Kansanshi produced 181,000 tonnes and Sentinel 189,000.
