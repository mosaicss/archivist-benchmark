UNP filed two 8-Ks on 2025-07-29. The Item 1.01 merger disclosure is in accession 0001193125-25-168150.

- **Cash per Norfolk Southern share: $88.82**, without interest. Each Norfolk Southern share also gets one Union Pacific share ([Item 1.01, Merger Consideration](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **Fee if regulatory approvals aren't obtained: $2.5 billion in cash**, paid by Union Pacific to Norfolk Southern in certain specified circumstances. Separately, either company would pay the other $2.5 billion if the deal ends because its board changes its recommendation or because of an alternative business combination proposal ([Item 1.01, Termination and Fees](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **End Date: January 28, 2028.** It extends automatically "under certain circumstances," which the 8-K summary does not spell out ([same passage](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
