- **Timing adjustments:** The MD&As also give figures adjusted for holiday timing. For Q3 2024 the adjusted figure is about 1.3%, excluding the effect of Thanksgiving. For Q4 2024 it is about 1.5%, after excluding a favourable timing effect (the snippet that names the cause is cut off). The table uses the headline figures instead.
- **Year-to-date figures:** Some MD&As also report a year-to-date number, which is easy to mistake for the quarter. Examples are 1.8% for the first 24 weeks of 2024, 1.3% for the first 40 weeks of 2024, and 2.9% for the first 24 weeks of 2025.
- **Data checks:** For Q1 2024 and Q1 2025, I read the numbers from the same-store sales table snippets, not from the narrative text. Both match the quarterly summary tables in later MD&As. The quarterly summary in the Q2 2026 MD&A shows the same values throughout (1.6, 3.5, 2.4, 2.2, 1.5, 2.5, 2.0, 0.5).
- **53rd week:** Q4 2025 was 13 weeks long, compared with 12 weeks in Q4 2024.
