**Mercury** is the closest planet to the Sun. It is also the smallest planet in the solar system, only a little larger than Earth's Moon ([NASA](<url:science.nasa.gov>)).

- **Average distance:** about 58 million km (36 million mi), or 0.4 AU ([Sky at Night Magazine](<url:www.skyatnightmagazine.com>)).
- **Distance range:** its orbit is the most stretched-out of any planet. It comes as close as about 46.0 million km and goes as far as about 69.8 million km ([Universe Today](<url:www.universetoday.com>)).
- **Not the hottest:** Venus is hotter than Mercury because of its thick atmosphere ([Sky at Night Magazine](<url:www.skyatnightmagazine.com>)).
