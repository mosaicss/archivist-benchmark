- CN filed two MD&As on each date, one in English and one in French. I used the English one each time, which is the version that contains the phrase "operating ratio."
- Some quarters also report an adjusted figure that is lower: Q2 2024 was 62.2% adjusted, Q1 2026 was 64.2% and Q2 2026 was 62.2%. The table uses the as-reported figures only.
- The MD&As also give year-to-date figures, such as 63.8% for the first half of 2024 and 63.6% for the first nine months of 2024. I left those out of the table.
- The figures agree across years. The prior-year numbers in each 2025 and 2026 MD&A match the ones reported a year earlier: 64.0%, 63.1%, 63.4% and 61.7%.
