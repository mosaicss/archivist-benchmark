Here are the answers, all from Costco's own filings.

**(a) Employees at the end of fiscal 2024 (10-K filed 2024-10-09):** 333,000 worldwide. That breaks down as 219,000 in the U.S., 53,000 in Canada and 61,000 in Other International. About 95% work in warehouses and distribution channels, and about 5% are represented by unions. [10-K, Human Capital – Employee Base](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)

**From the DEF 14A filed 2024-12-11, CEO pay ratio section** ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)):

- **(b) Employees at June 30, 2024:** About 328,000 worldwide, of which about 216,800 were in the U.S. and its territories. Employees in a few small countries were left out as allowed by SEC rules, so the median employee was picked from about 316,000 people.
- **(c) Ron Vachris's total compensation used for the ratio:** $12,354,831. This is higher than his $12,229,741 in the [Summary Compensation Table](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) because he became CEO on January 1, 2024, after Craig Jelinek left the role. For the ratio, his salary and non-equity incentive pay were adjusted to a full year.
- **(d) Median employee's total compensation:** $47,092.
- **(e) Pay ratio:** 262:1. Costco also gives a second ratio that counts only full-time workers: a median of $64,318 and a ratio of 192:1.
