Technic contributed about **15.5%** of Turkish Airlines' 2025 consolidated operating profit (13,928 ÷ 90,129).

These figures come from the 2025 Finansal Rapor filed 2026-03-04 (KAP doc 1565996). Amounts are in millions of TL.

**Consolidated, 2025**
- **Revenue (Hasılat): 955,472**, the "Toplam" column of the segment note ([Note 4.2](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)). I didn't separately pull the revenue line from the income statement.
- **Operating profit (Esas Faaliyet Karı): 90,129**, against 80,393 in 2024. This matches the income statement ([income statement](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)) and the segment total.

**Technic (Teknik) segment, 2025** ([Note 4.2](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
- **Total revenue: 107,220.** Of this, 26,857 came from outside the group and 80,363 from other segments. The 80,363 is removed in the 103,230 elimination, so Technic's own share of consolidated revenue is about 2.8%.
- **Operating profit: 13,928.** Aviation (Havacılık) made 76,201, and the elimination column for this line is zero.

**Technic's share of operating profit (my calculation)**
- 13,928 ÷ 90,129 ≈ **15.5%**.
- Because the elimination is zero, the two segments add up exactly to the consolidated figure.
- For comparison, 2024 was 6,843 ÷ 80,393 ≈ 8.5% ([2024 segment table](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
