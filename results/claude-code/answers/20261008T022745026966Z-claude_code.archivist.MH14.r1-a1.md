Here are the answers from First Quantum's Annual Information Form for the year ended December 31, 2025, filed 2026-02-10:

- **Ownership of Cobre Panamá:** The company owns **91.02%** of the mine ([Material Operating Projects table](<url:mosaic-finance.com>); [Ownership of properties](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)). The History section says the stake was 90% after November 2017; 91.02% is the current figure.
- **Approval announcement:** On **May 30, 2025**, the company announced that MPSA had received approval of the P&SM program for Cobre Panamá from the Government of Panama ([Risk factors, Cobre Panamá part 2/3](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **Funding:** The program is paid for by exporting **121,000 dry metric tonnes** of copper concentrate stored at the site ([same passage](<url:mosaic-finance.com>); [2025 history](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)). Another part of the same document says the program "continues to await approval from the Panamanian authorities," and that its timing affects whether that concentrate can be shipped ([part 1/3](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **2025 copper production:** The company produced **395,772 tonnes** of copper in 2025 ([Description of the Business](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)). Elsewhere it rounds this to about 396,000 tonnes ([Copper](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
