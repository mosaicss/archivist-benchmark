Power & Energy had the highest total sales of the three segments in 2025, according to Caterpillar's 10-K filed 2026-02-13 (MD&A, fiscal year ended Dec 31, 2025).

**Power & Energy in 2025:**
- Total sales: **$32.201B**, up 12% from $28.854B in 2024 ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
- Profit: **$6.418B**, up $682M or 12% ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
- Profit as a percent of total sales: **19.9%**, the same as in 2024 ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))

**2025 total sales by segment:**

| Segment | 2025 total sales |
|---|---|
| Power & Energy | $32.201B ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)) |
| Construction Industries | $25.060B ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)) |
| Resource Industries | $12.474B ([source](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)) |

The three add up to $69.735B. I calculated that sum myself; the filing doesn't report it. Segment total sales include sales between segments, so the sum is not the same as consolidated sales. For example, Power & Energy's total includes $5.058B of inter-segment sales.
