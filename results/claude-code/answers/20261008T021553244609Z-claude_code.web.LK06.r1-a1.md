Here are the figures from Lockheed Martin's FY2025 Form 10-K:

- **Total backlog at December 31, 2025:** **$193.6 billion**, up from $176.0 billion at December 31, 2024. Of that, $120.2 billion was funded.
- **Production F-35s delivered from program inception through December 31, 2025:** **1,293**. That's 927 F-35A, 238 F-35B and 128 F-35C, and the three add up to 1,293. Lockheed delivered a record 191 of them in 2025.
- **F-35 backlog at December 31, 2025:** **368 aircraft**.

I couldn't read the full 10-K directly. The SEC page only returned the cover and business sections. So these figures come from search-result excerpts that cite the filing, and the $193.6B also appears in Lockheed's Q4 2025 earnings release from the same day.
