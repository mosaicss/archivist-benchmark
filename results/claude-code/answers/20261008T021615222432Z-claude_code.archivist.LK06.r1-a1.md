Here are the three figures from Lockheed Martin's FY2025 Form 10-K, filed 2026-01-29:

- **Total backlog at December 31, 2025:** $193.6 billion, up from $176.0 billion at December 31, 2024. Funded backlog was $120.2 billion. ([Item 7, MD&A](<url:mosaic-finance.com>); the [Item 8 notes](<url:mosaic-finance.com>) give the same total.)
- **Production F-35s delivered from program inception through December 31, 2025:** 1,293 aircraft. That is 927 F-35A, 238 F-35B and 128 F-35C. ([Item 7, Aeronautics](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
- **F-35 backlog at that date:** 368 aircraft. ([same passage](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
