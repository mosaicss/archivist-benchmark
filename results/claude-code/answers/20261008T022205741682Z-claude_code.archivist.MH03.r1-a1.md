Here are the answers from Pfizer's 2025 Form 10-K, filed 2026-02-26:

- **Eliquis worldwide revenues in 2025:** $7,961 million, up from $7,366 million in 2024 (8% reported, 7% operational). U.S. revenues were $5,148 million and international revenues were $2,813 million. ([MD&A product discussion](<url:mosaic-finance.com>); [Note 17C](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
- **Share of total revenues:** 13%. The filing says "Eliquis accounted for 13% of Total revenues in 2025" ([Risk Factors](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)). This matches $7,961M divided by total revenues of $62,579M, which is about 12.7%.
- **Settled generic launch date (U.S.):** April 1, 2028. Pfizer and BMS settled with a number of generic companies, allowing them to launch on that date ([Patents footnote 5](<url:mosaic-finance.com>)).
- **Formulation patent expiry:** 2031. Pfizer won its case against three other generic companies, so they cannot launch until that patent expires. The composition of matter patent expires in November 2026. Both patents could still be challenged. If the formulation patent alone were held invalid, the settled companies could launch on November 21, 2026. If both patents were held invalid, they could launch immediately. ([same footnote](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
