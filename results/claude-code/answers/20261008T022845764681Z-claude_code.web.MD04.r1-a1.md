I found answers to all five parts. For (c)–(e), the Pegasus annual report PDF wouldn't open as readable text, so I couldn't check the wording against the report itself. Those answers come from news coverage of Pegasus's separate 11 Dec 2025 KAP notice and from a search summary that credits the annual report.

**(a) Transaction value: €154 million.** Reuters says this figure included both companies' debts. BloombergHT quotes the KAP text in Turkish as covering the whole Smartwings Group, *"temlike konu üstlenilecek alacaklar dahil"*, which means "including the receivables to be taken over by assignment." When the deal closed on 1 Oct 2026, the final price was €142.3 million.

**(b) Fleet: 47 aircraft.** Reuters and BloombergHT both describe this as the fleet flying under the Czech Airlines and Smartwings brands together.

**(c) Subsidiary: Pegasus Europe B.V.**, 100% owned by Pegasus and set up to buy the Smartwings Group shares.

**(d) Country: the Netherlands.**

**(e) Registration completed on 11 December 2025.** Setting it up was finished on 10 December 2025.
