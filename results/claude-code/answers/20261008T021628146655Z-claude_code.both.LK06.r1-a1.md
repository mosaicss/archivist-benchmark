These figures come from Lockheed Martin's FY2025 Form 10-K, filed 2026-01-29 (accession 0001628280-26-004195):

- **Total backlog at December 31, 2025:** $193.6 billion, up from $176.0 billion at December 31, 2024. Funded backlog was $120.2 billion of that. LMT expects about 37% of the backlog to turn into revenue within 12 months and about 60% within 24 months. ([MD&A, Item 7](<url:mosaic-finance.com>); the same total appears in [Item 8 notes](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
- **Production F-35s delivered from program inception through December 31, 2025:** 1,293 aircraft, made up of 927 F-35A, 238 F-35B and 128 F-35C. ([Item 7, Aeronautics](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
- **F-35 backlog at December 31, 2025:** 368 aircraft. The Lot 18–19 contract, definitized on September 29, 2025, added 3 Lot 18 and 148 Lot 19 aircraft to that backlog. ([same passage](<url:mosaic-finance.com>))
