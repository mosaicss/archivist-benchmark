**Lockheed Martin FY2025 (10-K for fiscal 2025, filed January 2026):**

- **F-35 share:** The 10-K says the F-35 program generated **27% of total consolidated sales** and **67% of Aeronautics' sales in 2025**. In 2024 the figures were 26% and 65%.
- **Aeronautics in 2025:** Net sales were **$30,257 million** and operating profit was **$2,086 million**, a margin of about 6.9%.
- **Total consolidated net sales in 2025:** $75,048 million.

**Implied F-35 sales:** about **$20.3 billion**.
- 67% × $30,257M ≈ $20,272M
- 27% × $75,048M ≈ $20,263M

The two methods agree within about $10M. Because the percentages are rounded, treat ~$20.3B as approximate.

I got the Aeronautics and total sales figures from Lockheed's Q4/full-year 2025 earnings release, which came out alongside the 10-K. The tool I used to read the 10-K couldn't pull its financial tables, so I didn't check those numbers against the 10-K itself.
