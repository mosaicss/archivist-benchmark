**Caveats:**
- **Sources:** I opened the 9M 2020, 6M 2022, 9M 2022, 6M 2023 and 6M 2024 MD&As myself and read the figures in them. The rest come from search summaries of the SEC filings.
- **Indirect or secondary sources:** I didn't open the Q1 2020 MD&A; its figure comes from the prior-year number in the Q1 2021 MD&A. The Q1 2023 and Q1 2024 figures come from press-release wording. The full-year 2025 figure is from a search summary, because the fetched 10-K text was cut off before the GMV sentence.
- **9M 2020 and 9M 2022:** These MD&As report $78.4B and $136.2B. Adding up the quarterly figures gives slightly different totals because of rounding and later updates.
- **6M 2023:** The MD&A says $104.6B in its overview but rounds to "$105 billion" in its GMV-definition section.
- **Q1 2025:** The 10-Q rounds the figure to $74.8B; the exact amount is about $74.75B.
